## bearing_turret

ComputerCraft aiming for Create Simulated gun bearings on the
Amazeballs pack.

Contract: `ainterface.json` at the repo root.

### Level

High-level. Specialized task script.

### Who

ComputerCraft program on the gun computer.

### What

Aim and fire a Simulated swivel-bearing turret.

### When

Radar, motors, and barrel are attached and the operator wants
engagement.

### Where

Amazeballs Minecraft world. ComputerCraft on that computer.

### Why

Create Radar cannot drive Simulated bearings. This topic is the
read, aim, and fire path that fills that gap.
